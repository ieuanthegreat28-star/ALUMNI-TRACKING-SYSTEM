HISTORY


  • The RIZAL EXPERIMENTAL STATION AND PILOT SCHOOL OF COTTAGE INDUSTRIES – LAS PIÑAS (RESPSCI-LP) was established by virtue of Republic Act 4571 which was approved on June 19, 1965. It started its formal operation in the municipality of Pasig on November 18, 1974, under the supervision of the Regional Director National Capital Region of the Ministry of Education, Culture and Sports. The school as a vocational institution provides training and livelihood courses in various places of the province of Rizal, Manila, Quezon City and Las Piñas. This caught the attention of the local officials of Las Piñas and offered a site at Moonwalk subdivision (the municipal motor pool then) to temporarily house the extension classes for the students from the nearby barangay in 1977.
