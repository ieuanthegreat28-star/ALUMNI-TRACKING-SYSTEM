Las Piñas City Technical-Vocational Highschool

In the succeeding years an open lot was offered at Mikesell Subdivision by the municipal official for a more appropriate site, however, it was short-lived for the extension classes due to the objections of homeowners. RESPSCI - LAS PIÑAS was temporarily house inside the chapel of Carmencita Village, Talon III, due to clamor of nearby residents and the barangay officials; the late Mayor Felimon C. Aguilar was able to provide a site for the school at Admiral Subdivision, Talon III, and he provided the first permanent structure a one storey 3 classroom (south wing bldg.)
